Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026

The Altar of the Circuit is a static page. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no cookies. What you write or choose stays in your browser.

Who is responsible

Sinaida Krivchenko, Prague, Czechia. For any privacy question, write to me through sinaida.eu/privacy.

What data

I do not collect personal data. Your reflections and answers are not sent to me or to anyone else. Any image you create is drawn in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA), which delivers pages through the Fastly network. When you open a page, the server receives a technical request: your IP address, the time, the address you asked for and your browser type. GitHub processes this to deliver the site and keep it secure. I do not collect or keep these logs.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in running and securing a public website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Transfers outside the EU: GitHub is a US company. It states that it relies on standard contractual clauses and takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

See the GitHub privacy statement.

Third parties

The page does not contact any third-party server. Fonts, scripts and the ambient sound are served from this same site or generated in your browser.

Local storage

The site stores one entry in your browser’s local storage: cookie-consent, with the value accepted or rejected, so the notice does not appear again. It stays on your device, is never transmitted, and remains until you clear the site data in your browser. The site sets no cookies.

Your rights

Your choices exist only in your own browser, so there is no record on my side to request or correct. For connection data held by GitHub you have the rights under GDPR: access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection. Write to me through sinaida.eu/privacy, and I pass your request on to GitHub where needed. You may also complain at any time to the Czech data protection authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, or to the authority in your own country.
